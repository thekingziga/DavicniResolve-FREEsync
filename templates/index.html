💡 DaVinci Resolve Path Mapping & Zero-Config Guide UNIVERSAL

Configure DaVinci Resolve once with Mapped Mounts so that clips across PC, Mac, and remote editors link automatically with zero manual relinking or red "Media Offline" clips.

⚡ 1. What is DaVinci Resolve "Mapped Mounts"?

DaVinci Resolve has a built-in virtual drive translator in its preferences. When projects are shared between Windows (D:\Users\...) and macOS (/Volumes/SSD/...), DaVinci swaps the disk prefix in memory. All timelines, audio waveforms, and color grades stay 100% online across every device!

🍎 2. Exact Setup on Mac (Laptops & External SSDs)

  1. Open DaVinci Resolve on your Mac.
  2. Press Cmd + , (or menu DaVinci Resolve → Preferences).
  3. In the left sidebar, click Media Storage.
  4. Scroll down to the Mapped Mounts table at the bottom and click Add:
    • Mapped Mount (Remote): D:\
    • Local Mount (Mac): /Volumes/YourSSD/D/ (or your chosen Download Folder)
  5. Click Save and reopen your project. Every clip links immediately!

👥 3. Remote Editor Workflow (Zero Friction)

When collaborating with an external editor:

  • Editor runs ResolveSync desktop app, connects to TrueNAS URL, and picks their local external drive as the Download Folder.
  • They configure one Mapped Mount in DaVinci: D:\ → E:\EditorFootage\D\ (or /Volumes/EditorSSD/D/ on Mac).
  • You can now collaborate over the PostgreSQL Network Project Server (thekingziga.com) or send DRP archives without missing a single media clip.

🔄 4. How ResolveSync Auto-Mirrors Drives

• Main PC File: D:\Users\zigav\Videos\clip.mp4
• TrueNAS Hub: /media/D/Users/zigav/Videos/clip.mp4 (Safe central storage)
• Mac / Laptop: /Volumes/SSD/D/Users/zigav/Videos/clip.mp4

Because the drive prefix D is kept as the root folder, mapping D:\ once connects 100% of footage regardless of where it was located on the original workstation.
